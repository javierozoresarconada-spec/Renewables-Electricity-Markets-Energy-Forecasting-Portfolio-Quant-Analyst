This is a public repository where I will share projects related to energy analytics, electricity markets, forecasting, and data science.

I hold an M.Sc. in Wind Energy from the Technical University of Denmark (DTU) and have around six years of professional experience in the 
wind energy sector, working as a Wind Energy Analyst at Ørsted and previously as a Technical Project Manager at Bladena ApS, both based in Denmark.

While my background is in wind energy analytics, I am currently expanding my expertise towards electricity markets, energy forecasting, 
quantitative analysis, and power trading. To support this transition, I am combining my industry experience with self-directed learning 
through high-quality educational resources, including publicly available university courses from institutions such as DTU.

This repository is a work in progress. My goal is to continuously develop and publish practical projects that demonstrate my learning 
journey on these topics.
